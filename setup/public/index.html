Canvas videos

Checking…

Spotify's looping Canvas clips in the widget need the sp_dc cookie from a browser you are logged into Spotify with. Skip this and the widget shows album art instead.

Where do I find sp_dc?
  1. Open open.spotify.com and log in.
  2. Press Shift + F9 for the Storage tab (or F12 → Application → Cookies).
  3. Under Cookies pick https://open.spotify.com, then copy the Value of sp_dc.

Widget themes

Design your own widget on a canvas - drag the album art, title, artist and progress bar wherever you want them. Saving turns it into a real theme you can switch to with !theme. The built-in themes stay untouched as fallbacks.

A theme per OBS scene

Give a scene a theme and Queueify switches to it the moment OBS cuts to that scene - a slim strip while you play, the big panel while you talk to chat. Scenes left on Leave as-is change nothing, so this is safe to fill in one scene at a time.
